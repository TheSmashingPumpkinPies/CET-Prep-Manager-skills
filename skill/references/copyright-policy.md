# Copyright & Data Governance Policy

## 1. Zero Direct Paper Distribution Principle
- The CET Prep Manager repository and Agent skills MUST NOT distribute raw copyrighted exam papers, audio tracks, or official answer keys.
- Real past papers possessed by learners (e.g. user-local PDFs, textbooks, past exam booklets) remain on the learner's local device.

## 2. Metadata & Analytics Boundaries
- Storing performance metadata (exam year/month, paper number, question-level correct/wrong counts, completion duration) in the local SQLite database is fully permissible.
- Storing student-authored essays and student-authored translations for evaluation and longitudinal progress tracking is strictly private and local.

## 3. Generated Practice Labeling
- Any AI-generated practice drills (listening scripts, cloze exercises, sample essay prompts, translation practice paragraphs) must be explicitly identified as **"Original AI-generated CET-style practice"**, never mislabeled as official past CET exam questions.
